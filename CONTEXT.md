# Partick Victoria Park Church website

The public website for Partick Victoria Park Church: where visitors find out about the church, and where members catch up on services and news.

## Language

### Content

**Service Recording**:
A video of a whole Sunday morning service, published on YouTube, together with its date, Sermon Title, and the Bible Passages preached on. Other services are not recorded. Each one is created automatically from the YouTube channel with just its video and date; an Editor adds the Sermon Title and Bible Passages afterwards. Until then it is an **Incomplete** Service Recording, but it is still shown on the site.
_Avoid_: Sermon (as a content type), Video, Recording

**Sermon Title**:
The name given to the sermon preached within a Service Recording, and its headline. It is optional; without one, the service date is the headline.
_Avoid_: Title (on its own), Name

**Bible Passage**:
A reference to a portion of scripture, such as "Mark 15:1-20". A Service Recording has one, and sometimes two.
_Avoid_: Reading, Verse, Scripture

**Series**:
An optional label that groups Service Recordings preached as a sequence on one theme or book. A Service Recording belongs to at most one Series. A Series has no existence beyond its name: recordings whose Series name matches exactly belong together.
_Avoid_: Category, Theme, Tag

**Page**:
Any piece of written content on the site that isn't a Service Recording, whether permanent ("Safeguarding") or topical ("Christmas services"). There is no separate news or article type.
_Avoid_: Article, Post, News item

**Menu Page**:
A Page that appears in the site's main menu, in an order the editors choose.
_Avoid_: Nav item, Section

**Published**:
Whether a Service Recording or Page appears on the site. Everything is Published unless an Editor switches it off; switching it off hides the item without deleting it.
_Avoid_: Live (that means the Live Stream), Visible, Draft

**Live Stream**:
The Sunday morning service as it is being broadcast on YouTube. Once the service ends it becomes a video that an Editor turns into a Service Recording.
_Avoid_: Live service, Broadcast

**Gallery**:
The single place on the site for a small, Editor-chosen set of photos, each with a caption and a description for people who can't see it.
_Avoid_: Photos page, Album

**Contact Message**:
A message a visitor sends through the site's contact form. It is delivered to the church by email and not stored on the site.
_Avoid_: Enquiry, Submission, Form entry

**Featured Page**:
A Page shown on the home page. It stays featured until an editor un-features it; nothing expires automatically.
_Avoid_: Notice, Pinned post, Announcement

### People

**Editing Guide**:
The single printable page that teaches an Editor everything they need, together with the help text on each field in the editor.
_Avoid_: Manual, Docs, Instructions

**Editor**:
A church member invited by email to add and change Service Recordings and Pages. Editors cannot change how the site is built or invite anyone.
_Avoid_: Admin, User, Author

**Maintainer**:
A person with access to the site's code, who can invite Editors and change the site's structure. Usually just one person; Editors never need a Maintainer to change content.
_Avoid_: Admin, Developer, Owner, Webmaster
