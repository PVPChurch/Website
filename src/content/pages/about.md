Partick Victoria Park Church is a Church of Scotland congregation at 259 Dumbarton Road, Partick, Glasgow.

## Sunday worship

We meet for worship every Sunday at **11am** and **6.30pm**. Everyone is welcome.

The 11am service is streamed live on our YouTube channel, and recordings of past services are on the [Service recordings](/recordings/) page.

## Baptisms, weddings and funerals

If you would like to ask about a baptism, wedding or funeral, please [get in touch](/contact/) and we'll be glad to help.
