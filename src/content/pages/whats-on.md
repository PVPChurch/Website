Everything below happens at the church, 259 Dumbarton Road, unless it says otherwise. Everyone is welcome.

## Sunday

- **Morning worship**, 11am. Live streamed on YouTube.
- **Young Church and Crèche**, 11am. Children join the start of the service in the Sanctuary for a short time of worship and a children's talk, then go to their own room for activities.
- **Sunday Community Café**, 5pm to 6pm. Free soup, sandwiches and tea or coffee.
- **Evening worship**, 6.30pm.

## Monday

- **Craft Group**, 2pm to 4pm.

## Tuesday

- **Prayer Meeting**, 1pm to 2pm.

## Wednesday

- **Wednesday Fellowship**, 10.30am.

## Thursday

- **Baby and Toddler Group**, 9.30am to 11.30am.
- **Vocal Dynamics Community Choir**, 5.30pm to 7pm.

## Other days

- **Broken Voices**, a singing and fellowship group led by John Carslaw, our Mission and Outreach Co-ordinator. It meets on different days, so please [get in touch](/contact/) to find out when.
